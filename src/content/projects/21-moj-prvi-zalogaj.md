# Challenge

Moj prvi zalogaj combines educational content, recipes, books, courses, and a live WooCommerce shop. The production site needed ongoing technical maintenance and performance work while continuing to support active commerce and marketing campaigns.

The store also needed business-specific promotion and recovery behavior that was not covered by its Astra Pro and Elementor presentation layer alone.

## Role and responsibilities

I maintain and optimize the WordPress and WooCommerce implementation. My responsibilities include:

- Maintaining the Astra Pro and Elementor storefront and resolving production issues.
- Improving frontend and WordPress performance as the content and product catalog evolve.
- Supporting WooCommerce product, cart, and checkout behavior.
- Integrating and maintaining the custom abandoned-cart recovery and Portikla BOGO plugins used by the website and at company level.

## Technical decisions

### Preserve the existing WordPress composition

Astra Pro and Elementor remain the presentation foundation, allowing existing editorial and commerce content to continue operating while maintenance and optimization are delivered incrementally.

### Package business rules as custom plugins

Abandoned-cart recovery and BOGO promotion logic were implemented as separate WooCommerce plugins rather than embedding business rules in the theme. This keeps the functionality reusable at company level and independent from visual page-builder changes.

### Optimize around a live commerce system

Maintenance and performance work is carried out without replacing the active WooCommerce catalog, checkout, recipe, blog, and course journeys already used by the business.

## Result or measurable impact

The website remains in active production with its content, product catalog, cart, and checkout journeys supported by ongoing maintenance and optimization. Two custom WooCommerce plugins now provide reusable abandoned-cart recovery and BOGO promotion capabilities.

No verified performance benchmark or conversion metric was supplied, so none is claimed here.
