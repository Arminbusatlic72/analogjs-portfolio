# Challenge

Royal Swinkels needed a new B2B customer storefront built on its SAP Commerce and Spartacus platform.

The frontend theme had to be developed from the ground up with attention to performance and scalability, while Gigya login and registration needed to be integrated into the Composable Storefront experience.

## Role and responsibilities

I contributed to the Royal Swinkels storefront from January through May 2024 as part of Elision's delivery team. My documented responsibilities included:

- Contributing to the storefront theme from its initial implementation.
- Developing frontend functionality with Angular and Spartacus.
- Working with Hybris CMS-backed commerce content.
- Integrating Gigya login and registration into the Composable Storefront.
- Supporting performance and scalability concerns during development.

## Technical decisions

### SAP Spartacus as the storefront layer

The project was built on the client's SAP Hybris commerce platform. Spartacus provided the Angular-based storefront architecture needed to consume and present that commerce functionality.

### Gigya login and registration within the storefront

Gigya authentication was integrated directly into the Composable Storefront so login and registration remained part of the main B2B customer experience rather than a separate interface.

The available project material does not document additional architectural decisions, so none are inferred here.

## Result or measurable impact

A new B2B storefront theme was developed and Gigya login and registration were integrated into the SAP Composable Storefront.
